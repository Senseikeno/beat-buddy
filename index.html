Welcome to Beat Buddy!

1. Tap the sound buttons to choose instruments.
2. Tap squares in the beat grid to build a rhythm.
3. Press Play and change the speed.
4. Save your song, or record and export it.

Tip: The piano keys play notes whenever you tap them. Have fun experimenting!
